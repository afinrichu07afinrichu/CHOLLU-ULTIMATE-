CHOLLU Privacy Notice

This is a starter notice and must be reviewed and completed by the app owner before public launch.

Information handled

CHOLLU may process account usernames, email addresses, profile details, chat messages, group membership, reports, notification subscriptions and basic service timestamps to provide app features.

Storage and service providers

Data is stored using the Cloudflare services configured by the app operator. Optional AI, email, push notification and call services may process data needed to provide those features.

Safety and retention

Users should avoid sharing highly sensitive information. Message deletion and disappearing-message settings may not remove copies already saved by recipients or backups.
